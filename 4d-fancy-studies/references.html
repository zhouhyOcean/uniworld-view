Source provenance

The source column shows the exact prepared input to our pipeline; original independent input files are linked separately. Author-generated novel views and side-by-side result montages are not used as input. Some originals are synthetic. These qualitative examples do not establish paired-view ground truth or fair benchmark superiority.

For mammoths, the surfing otter and the dragon procession, independent Sora publisher originals were matched to TrajectoryCrafter’s source panels. The original gallery preserves this identification record. The original source camera is fitted from each clip; target routes are authored independently. Camera records are requested/renderer-recorded controls, not recovered output poses.

All generated frames are retained in their original order; public playback matches the sampled source cadence, and raw 8fps worker outputs remain in the reproduction package. No frame interpolation or source looping. Novel surfaces, animated anatomy, smoke, water and cloth can be approximate.
